Projects across the Nordics

A selection of the homes and buildings EstNor has delivered — mainly to Norway, alongside Sweden, Estonia and beyond.

Oslo, Norway

Oslo apartment building

A multi-storey apartment building delivered to Oslo using EstNor's factory-finished modules. Modules were completed inside and out — including wiring, plumbing and heating — before shipping, then joined together on site to form the finished building, cutting on-site construction time far below a conventional build.
Norway

Private log home

A turn-key handcrafted log house, built to Norwegian log-house regulations, for a private client in Norway.

Sweden

Element-built family home

A large family home built using factory-produced exterior and interior elements, assembled on a Swedish site.

Estonia

Cottage development

A cluster of modular cottages produced in Kiili and delivered ready-finished to an Estonian coastal site.
