Hand-hewn pine, built to Norwegian tradition

A steady hand and a sharp eye — our log house specialists were trained by Norwegian log-house teachers, and follow Norwegian requirements and rules on every build.

Craftsmanship

A creational, hand-built craft

Building a handcraft log house is a highly creational process that demands a steady hand and a sharp eye. Over the years, EstNor has assembled a team of the best log house specialists — trained directly by Norwegian log-house teachers.

We follow the log house requirements and building rules effective in Norway when choosing materials and constructing a house, honouring Norwegian building traditions shaped over thousands of years.

Construction detail

Our log houses are hand-made with hewed edges, using pine. The sides are cut off from the round log, leaving a remaining wall thickness of 200mm — a construction method chosen for strength, insulation and character alike.

Order options

Turn-key, or log-parts kit

Turn-key solution

EstNor manages the full build — from hand-hewn log walls through to a finished, move-in-ready log house.

Log-parts kit

We supply the handcraft log walls, bulkhead beams, roof purlins and figured stand posts — for building teams who want to assemble on site themselves.
