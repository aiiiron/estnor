Furnishing your new home

A house is only finished once it's furnished. EstNor helps subscribers furnish their new homes from the very first day.

Useful information

Furniture, sourced for you

Beyond building the house itself, EstNor offers to procure furniture for house subscribers who want a turn-key, move-in-ready result.

We cooperate closely with Artex Design Factory to source furniture that matches the character of an EstNor timber home — from kitchens and built-in storage to finishing pieces for living spaces.

Interested subscribers can discuss furniture packages alongside their house order, so that interior planning happens in step with construction rather than after the keys are handed over.
