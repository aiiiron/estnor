About EstNor

Factory production with 25 years of experience

EstNor OÜ is an Estonian company with its own house factory in Kiili near Tallinn. Since 2000, EstNor has manufactured prefabricated timber houses as well as facade and roof elements for new build and renovation.

25 yearsof experience in prefabricated timber element construction
1,000+residential units built
≈10,000 m²production floor area per year
90 %export share (Norway, Sweden, DACH region and more)
What EstNor brings

Manufacturing, not advisory services

  • Digital planning & engineering of prefabricated houses and facade elements
  • Series production under factory conditions with consistent quality
  • Integration of windows, insulation, ventilation routing and surface within the element
  • Building survey, structural design, transport and installation support on request
  • Annual output of around 50 houses, or ≈10,000 m² of floor area
Market & origin

Why from Estonia?

Estonia has an established timber and element construction industry with long export experience. Around 90 % of EstNor's production goes to export — mainly to Norway, and also to Sweden, Switzerland, Finland, Germany and Liechtenstein. As an EU member state there are no customs formalities in trade with the rest of the EU.

Short history

How EstNor was built

  1. 2000EstNor OÜ is founded on 20 October. A former fertiliser storage building in Kiili is purchased and rebuilt into our first manufacturing facility.
  2. 2001The very first handcrafted timber house leaves the new factory — the start of EstNor's production.
  3. 2002The team grows to five employees as demand for our houses increases.
  4. 2003A hoist crane is installed on the production line, sharply increasing manufacturing efficiency.
  5. 2022EstNor becomes the first participating manufacturer in the Estonian KredEx pilot programme for serial renovation, supplying the largest volume of facade elements for twelve apartment buildings.
  6. TodayEstNor produces roughly 50 houses a year — around 10,000 m² of floor area — and manufactures prefabricated facade elements for serial renovation, both certified to ISO 9001, CE and EOTA.
