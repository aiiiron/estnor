Two decades of building in timber

EstNor OÜ was founded on 20 October 2000 in Kiili, Estonia. Since then we've grown into a certified manufacturer of element, log and modular houses, shipping mostly to Norway.

Company profile

An Estonian manufacturer built for Nordic export

EstNor is an Estonian capital-based company whose main field of activity is the manufacturing and production of wood houses. Our factory and offices are located at Kurna tee 33 in Kiili, Harju County — a short drive from Tallinn.

Over more than two decades we have built over 1,000 living units. Norway remains our primary export market, receiving roughly 80–90% of our production, followed by Sweden, Estonia, Finland, Switzerland, Germany and Liechtenstein.

EstNor employs 52 full-time staff and has successfully passed annual ETA and CE certification audits, giving us the right to issue the CE mark on our certified products.

Short history

How EstNor was built

2000

EstNor OÜ is founded on 20 October. A former fertiliser storage building in Kiili is purchased and rebuilt into our first manufacturing facility.

2001

The very first handcrafted log house leaves the new factory — the start of our log-building tradition.

2002

The team grows to five employees as demand for our houses increases.

2003

A hoist crane is installed on the production line, sharply increasing manufacturing efficiency.

Today

EstNor produces roughly 50 houses a year — around 10,000 m² of floor area — with 52 employees and CE/ETA-certified production across element, log and modular houses.
