Why factory-built timber

Built indoors, finished right

Producing indoors, in a controlled environment, removes the variables — rain, snow, wind, temperature — that damage a house or facade built on an open site. Elements are produced to millimetre accuracy, so on-site assembly is fast and the finished building performs exactly as designed.

What that means in practice
  • Consistent quality from repeatable factory conditions
  • Weather-independent production, year-round
  • Far less time, noise and disruption on site
  • Renovation possible while a building stays occupied

Serial renovation

Serial renovation starts in the factory, not on the scaffolding

EstNor manufactures insulated facade and roof elements with windows, ventilation routing and a finished surface already built in. Around 90 % of the facade work happens under controlled factory conditions — on-site installation then takes weeks instead of months.
