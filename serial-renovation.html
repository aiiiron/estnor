Renovation with factory-produced elements

The same factory precision we bring to new houses can give an ageing building a new envelope — faster, and with far less disruption for residents.

How serial renovation works

Upgrade, without moving out

Instead of scaffolding a building for months, EstNor measures the existing structure and manufactures made-to-fit facade, roof and insulation elements indoors — to the same millimetre accuracy we use for new builds.

Finished elements are delivered to site and fitted directly onto the existing structure, dramatically shortening the time residents live with construction work around them, while improving insulation, weatherproofing and appearance.

It's a practical way to extend the life of older housing stock without the cost and disruption of a full rebuild.

Benefits

Why factory-made renovation elements

Faster on site

Most of the work happens in the factory before delivery — cutting weeks off a traditional renovation timeline.

Less disruption

Residents can often stay in place, with far less scaffolding, noise and dust than a conventional retrofit.

Better performance

New insulated facade and roof elements improve energy efficiency and weatherproofing for decades to come.

Have a renovation project?

Tell us about the building and we'll assess whether serial renovation is a fit.
